Let's settle your body first 🌊

When we feel nervous, our body speeds up. Slow breathing tells it "we're okay." Follow the circle.

Ready?

Still buzzy? Try grounding — find these around you:

  • 5 things you can see
  • 4 things you can touch
  • 3 things you can hear
  • 2 things you can smell
  • 1 thing you can taste (or one slow breath)

What would you do? 🎭

There's no perfect answer — practicing in your head makes the real thing feel easier. Pick what feels most like you.
